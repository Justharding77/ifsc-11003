About Us

Our secret? It's not one, admittedly. The best and freshest ingredients are a big part of what makes our food special. Another part is the love and passion we have for comfort food and bringing people together through flavorful meals.

At MeMe's Twisted Potato and More LLC, we focus on creating vegan dishes that are full of flavor, colorful, satisfying, and comforting. Every meal is prepared with care and creativity to make sure customers enjoy every bite.

We believe vegan food should never feel boring or plain. Our goal is to give customers a unique experience while still serving foods that feel familiar and homemade.
